🏥 Health & Emergency

Trusted health information, emergency guidance and official health resources.

🚨 National Emergency Number

112

Tap to call for urgent emergencies in Nigeria. If the call does not connect, go to the nearest hospital or contact local emergency responders.

🩹 First Aid Basics

  • Keep yourself and the injured person away from danger.
  • Call 112 for a life-threatening emergency.
  • For heavy bleeding, apply firm direct pressure with a clean cloth.
  • Do not move someone with a possible neck or back injury unless there is immediate danger.
  • Do not give food, drink or medicine to an unconscious person.

⚠️ When to seek urgent help

Seek emergency help for difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, chest pain, stroke signs, seizures, unconsciousness, serious burns or major injuries.

🧼 Everyday Prevention

Wash hands with soap, drink safe water, use mosquito nets, keep vaccinations up to date and seek advice from a qualified health worker when unwell.
